# PART B — GRADUATED APPROACH / APDR CYCLE (4 NATIONS)

> **Compiled 5 July 2026.** STATUTORY GUIDANCE (Codes) and STATUTORY (Act/Order + Regs) for each nation. All URLs verified live.

---

## B1 — What the APDR / equivalent cycle is in each nation

| Nation | National term | Statutory anchor | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **England** | "Graduated approach" with 4 stages: **Assess → Plan → Do → Review** | SEND CoP §§6.44–6.56 (schools); §§5.38–5.44 (EYFS); CFA 2014 s.66 | STATUTORY GUIDANCE |
| **Wales** | **IDP cycle**: decide ALN → prepare IDP → maintain IDP → review IDP (within person-centred practice) | ALN Act 2018 ss.10–11, 13, 21–24; ALN Code Chs 9–11, 21, 23, 25 | STATUTORY + STATUTORY GUIDANCE |
| **Scotland** | **Staged intervention** (universal → targeted → specialist) leading to a **Co-ordinated Support Plan (CSP)** for the most complex cases; within the wider **GIRFEC** framework | asp 4 ss.1, 2, 5, 9–11; SSI 2005/518; Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 s.3 (Named Person) | STATUTORY |
| **Northern Ireland** | **Five-Stage approach** (Stages 1–3 school-based, 4–5 EA-led) | NI SEN CoP §§1.7–1.9; SEN Regs (NI) 2005 | STATUTORY GUIDANCE (Code) + STATUTORY (Regs) |

> **GIRFEC (Scotland):** Getting It Right For Every Child is the overarching children-wellbeing policy; uses the **SHANARRI** wellbeing indicators and the **My World Triangle/World of Wellbeing** tools. Named in asp 4 s.1(5A) (inserted by 2016 Act).

## B2 — Phase-by-phase breakdown

### B2.1 ENGLAND (SEND CoP 2015 paras 5.38–5.44, 6.44–6.56)

| Phase | What must be recorded | Who is responsible | Review cadence | Good evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Assess** | Clear analysis of needs; teacher assessment; previous progress/attainment; national data comparison; parents' views; **pupil's own views**; external professional advice | Class/subject teacher + SENCO; specialists (EP, SALT, OT, CAMHS, specialist teachers for HI/VI/PD) "where parents agree" | Initial at identification; then "regularly reviewed" (5.44) | Baseline data on attainment vs age-related expectations; developmental milestones (EYFS); standardised assessment; Boxall profile; historic attendance/exclusion; external reports |
| **Plan** | Agreed outcomes sought; interventions and support; expected impact; clear review date; evidence-based intervention; staff skills; **recorded on the school's information system** (6.49) | SENCO in consultation with class teacher, parent and pupil | Recorded at start of each cycle; review date set as part of plan | SMART outcomes (CoP 9.66); named intervention with frequency/duration/owner; baseline recorded; one of 4 broad areas named |
| **Do** | Implementation of interventions; class teacher retains day-to-day responsibility; SENCO supports problem-solving | Class/subject teacher (day-to-day); SENCO (oversight) | Daily delivery; ongoing assessment of child's response | Delivery log: who, what, when, for how long; child's response; adjustments; staff training delivered |
| **Review** | Impact and quality evaluated; pupil and parent views; revised outcomes/support agreed | Class teacher + SENCO + parent + pupil | **At least every 12 months** for EHCP (5.46, 6.56); "increasingly detailed and more frequent review" for SEN Support | Quantitative progress data; qualitative evidence; **decision**: continue / adapt / escalate to EHCNA request |

**Longitudinal data that best evidences each phase (England):**
- **Assess:** P-levels / national curriculum year expectations; on-entry baseline; developmental milestones (EYFS); YARC, NELI; Boxall; historic attendance/exclusion; historic EHCP / external reports.
- **Plan:** PIVAT/BSquared; IEP / Provision Map entries with dates, owner, frequency.
- **Do:** Daily/weekly delivery records; TA timetables; intervention attendance; home–school communication.
- **Review:** Termly tracking vs targets; P-scores or progress-from-baseline graphs; scaled scores; behavioural/incident data; pupil/parent voice records; next-step planning.

### B2.2 WALES (ALN Code 2021 Chs 9–11, 21, 23, 25)

Wales does not use "APDR" terminology. The equivalent is built into the **statutory IDP process**: decide ALN → prepare IDP → maintain IDP → review IDP (ALN Act 2018 ss.10, 11, 13, 21–24). Code Ch 22 (person-centred practice, ¶¶22.6–22.16) is the practice frame.

| Phase | What must be recorded | Who is responsible | Review cadence | Good evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deciding ALN** | Evidence of progress over time; standardised assessment; observations; ALNCo analysis; parent/child views; multi-agency advice; reasons for ALN decision including any differences of opinion (Code 23.28) | ALNCo in school / FEI named ALN lead / LA (for under-3s, looked-after, out-of-school) | Continuous; the IDP must be reviewed at least once in the review period — **12 months** for an LA-maintained IDP (or earlier if requested) — Code Ch 25 | Whole-school tracking; PIVAT/BSquared progress; standardised assessments; attendance/exclusion; therapy reports; CAMHS/paediatric reports; parent/child views using person-centred headings |
| **Preparing IDP** | Annex A: 1A (profile), 2A (description of ALN), 2B (ALP per element: intended outcome, ALP, Welsh-language provision, organisation, start/end dates, rationale), 2C (NHS-secured ALP), 2D (named placement) | Body preparing the IDP: maintained school, FEI, or LA | The IDP must set a "date before which the IDP must be reviewed" (1B.2) and a "proposed review date" (1B.3) | SMART outcomes (Code 23.30–23.32); rationale for each ALP element; s.3A contributions from all relevant agencies |
| **Delivering ALP** | Delivery of each ALP element (2B.2 / 2C.2) by the named provider (2B.4 / 2C.4); progress monitoring by the maintaining body | Named organisation in 2B.4 / 2C.4; ALNCo oversight | Daily/termly; Code 25.6 (under review) | Delivery records; mid-cycle tracking; child/parent feedback during the year |
| **Reviewing IDP** | Code Ch 25 sets a "review period" prescribed by regulations; the maintaining body "must" review on request from child/parent/YP or NHS body (25.21–25.28). Default timescales: **35 school days / 35 term-time days / 7 weeks** respectively for a requested review (Code 25.23) | Maintaining body (school/FEI/LA) | At least annually for IDPs; on request; on significant change in circumstances | Updated tracking; outcomes met/not-met; child/parent voice; updated person-centred profile; amended outcomes & ALP; recorded conclusions |

**Longitudinal data that best evidences each phase (Wales):** identical categories to England, framed within the Annex A standard-form sections.

### B2.3 SCOTLAND (asp 4 + SSI 2005/518)

| Phase | What must be recorded | Who is responsible | Review cadence | Good evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Identification of ASN** | s.5(1) "general functions" — identify and meet ASN. SSI 2005/518 reg 3(1)(b)(iii) requires a "profile of the skills and capabilities" | Education authority; pre-school health/early-years | Continuous; authority required to "keep under consideration" (s.6(1)) | Tracking data, attainment, observations, child/parent views, multi-agency advice |
| **Plan (CSP)** | Where the s.2 test is met (complex/multiple factors, likely to continue >1 year, require significant additional support from education + ≥1 other agency), the authority must prepare a **CSP** (s.9). The CSP must contain (s.9(2)): educational objectives; additional support required; persons to provide it; nominated school; named contact officers | Education authority; Additional Support Co-ordinator (ASC) named in the plan | Set review timetable recorded in the CSP (reg 3(1)(b)(v)) | SMART educational objectives (s.9(2)(a)(ii)); clear identification of contributing agencies; risk assessment where relevant |
| **Do** | Delivery of the support named in the CSP; authority must involve appropriate agencies under ss.12–13 and may request help under s.23 | Named persons in s.9(2)(a)(iv) and the ASC | Daily/termly; ASC co-ordinates | Delivery logs; mid-cycle notes; professional input recorded |
| **Review** | s.10 requires review: (a) on the expiry of **12 months** from the date the plan was prepared; and (b) on the expiry of each successive period of 12 months from the previous review. Earlier reviews permitted on significant change or on request from parent/YP (s.10(3)–(5)). SSI 2005/518 reg 5 requires review to be completed within **12 weeks** of the applicable expiry date, with permitted extension up to 20 weeks. Authority must decide to continue (with or without amendment) or discontinue (s.10(6)) | Education authority; parent/YP can request earlier review (s.10(4)–(5)) | **Annual** statutory review (s.10(2)); on request; on significant change | Updated profile; progress vs objectives; child/YP views; agency contributions; recorded decision |

**Longitudinal data that best evidences each phase (Scotland):** Holistic Pupil Profiles / CfE tracking data; SHANARRI; progress against CSP educational objectives; multi-agency review notes; pupil/parent voice.

### B2.4 NORTHERN IRELAND (NI SEN CoP Oct 2025 + SEN Regs (NI) 2005)

NI uses a **5-stage approach** rather than a 4-phase APDR; stages 1–3 school-based, 4–5 EA-led. Code is explicit: "at the heart of the work of every school and every class lies a cycle of planning, teaching and assessing" (2.1).

| Phase | What must be recorded | Who is responsible | Review cadence | Good evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1 – initial identification** (Code 1.8, 2.45) | Concern raised; child registered; initial action; information shared with SENCO | Class teacher + SENCO | Continuously; "register" updated | Initial teacher concern, baseline, parent informed |
| **Stage 2 – SENCO-led action** (1.8, 2.49–2.51) | SENCO collects/records information; co-ordinates provision; draws up an **Individual Education Plan (IEP)** with **targets, monitoring and review date** (2.49–2.50) | SENCO in consultation with class teacher and parents | "Review at the agreed date" (2.50); review outcomes: child moves to Stage 3, or to a longer review period (2.51) | IEP with SMART targets, intervention type/frequency, monitoring method |
| **Stage 3 – external specialists** (1.8) | Advice from outside specialists (EP, specialist teachers); external advice informs next steps | SENCO + outside specialists | Reviewed at intervals consistent with child's needs | External professional reports incorporated; reviewed targets |
| **Stage 4 – statutory assessment** (1.8; Part III) | EA decides whether a statutory assessment is needed; seeks advice on educational, medical, psychological, social services (Regs Part II) | EA (Board) | Decision within statutory time limit | Multi-disciplinary advice; parental representations; child views; Stages 1–3 evidence |
| **Stage 5 – statement** (1.8; Part IV) | Statement issued; **annual review** required (Code 6.1–6.6; Regs Part III); 2-monthly target setting; transition review at **age 14+** (Code 6.34–6.35) | EA (Board) | **Annual review** (Code 6.1); transition plan from age 14 | Progress vs statement objectives; review report; parental representations; updated statement/amendment notice |

**Longitudinal data that best evidences each phase (NI):** IEP review records; standardised assessment scores; Stage 1–3 action evidence (needed for Stage 4); annual review reports; transition plan outcomes (Yrs 10–11).

## B3 — Standard field structure of the SEN Support Plan / equivalent

| Field | England (SEN Support plan) | Wales (School-maintained IDP — Annex A) | Scotland (CSP) | Northern Ireland (Statement of SEN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Statutory status** | Good practice (Code 6.49); no prescribed form | **STATUTORY** form at Annex A of the ALN Code | **STATUTORY** form at Schedule to SSI 2005/518 | **STATUTORY** form (Code 4.17; Regs Part III r.15) |
| **Statutory anchors** | C&FA 2014 ss.6, 66, 77; SEND Regs 2014 | ALN Act 2018 ss.10, 12, 13; ALN Code Annex A | asp 4 s.9; SSI 2005/518 reg 3 | Education (NI) Order 1996 art.16; SEN Regs (NI) 2005 r.15 |
| **Header / identity** | Pupil name, DOB, UPN, year, setting | 1A.1 Full name, 1A.2 Likes to be known as, 1A.3 DOB, 1A.4 Gender & preferred pronouns, 1A.5 Current setting, 1A.6 Home address, 1A.7 Parents' names, 1A.8 Email, 1A.9 Parents' tel, 1A.10 Communication reqs, 1A.11 Consent (YP), 1A.12 Capacity | Unique Pupil Identifier; full name, home address, contact tel, DOB, gender, preferred language/form of communication, school, date of entry | Part 1: Name, address, DOB, home language, religion; names/addresses of parents/guardians/carers |
| **Profile (about me)** | Captured in school system; no prescribed format | **1C Profile (About me)** — character, gifts and talents, what's important, best way to support; person-centred headings: "What is important to the learner / What is important for the learner / What is working and should be built on / What is not working and needs to change" (Code 23.24) | "Profile" — "skills and capabilities … and any other relevant information" (reg 3(1)(b)(iii)) | Not a separate section in statement; "ascertainable wishes of the child should be considered" (Code 1.7) |
| **Strengths / needs / outcomes** | Outcomes, expected impact, interventions, staff skills (Code 6.48) | 2A Description of ALN; 2B Description & delivery of ALP per element with sub-fields 2B.1 Intended outcome, 2B.2 ALP, 2B.3 Welsh?, 2B.4 Organisation/contact, 2B.5 Start date, 2B.6 End/review date, 2B.7 Rationale | s.9(2)(a)(i) factors giving rise to ASN; (ii) educational objectives; (iii) additional support required; (iv) persons providing support | Part 2: All learning difficulties; Part 3: Provision (objectives, provision, monitoring) |
| **Health provision** | Captured if contributing to outcomes | **2C** ALP to be secured by an NHS body (2C.1–2C.7 mirror 2B) | "additional support" may be health, social work, etc. via s.23 requests | Part 5: Non-educational needs; Part 6: Non-educational provision |
| **Social care** | Captured if contributing | (Health sec 2C; social care in narrative) | via s.23 requests to other agencies | Part 5/6 as above |
| **Placement** | Captured at school level | **2D** Places: 2D.1 named maintained school in Wales; 2D.2 particular school/institution; 2D.3 board and lodging | s.9(2)(b) "a nomination of a school" | Part 4: type/name of school |
| **Person(s) responsible / contacts** | SENCO named | 1B.1 Organisation maintaining the IDP; 1B.2 Date before which review required; 1B.3 Proposed review date | Schedule: Additional Support Provision Co-ordinator (s.9(2)(c)); Parental Advice and Information Officer (s.9(2)(d)) | Board maintains |
| **Pupil/parent/YP views** | "Plans should take into account the views of the child" (5.40); parents involved (5.41) | Code 23.26 — views recorded **separately from** parents; child/YP quoted directly | "any views … expressed by the child or young person and by a parent" (reg 3(1)(b)(iv)) | Part 1 names child/parent; Code 1.7: ascertainable wishes considered |
| **Signatures / authorisation** | Headteacher / SENCO | Duly authorised officer | "Dated and signed by a duly authorised officer of the education authority" (reg 3(1)(c)) | Signature and date (Code 4.17) |
| **Appendices** | Specialist reports, evidence of graduated approach | **Part 3** 3A Record of information used; 3B Timeline of key events; 3C Transition; 3D Travel | The plan itself; documents may be cross-referenced | Appendices: 1) parental reps; 2) educational advice; 3) medical; 4) psychological; 5) social services; 6) any other (including child's views) (Code 4.18) |
