## A2 — DEFINITION OF SEN / ALN / ASN AND THE LEGAL TEST

### A2.1 ENGLAND — SEN definition (verbatim, CFA 2014 s.20(1))

> "(1) A child or young person has special educational needs if he or she has a learning difficulty or disability (whether or not he or she has any other learning difficulty or disability) which calls for special educational provision to be made for him or her.
>
> (2) A child ... has a learning difficulty or disability if he or she —
>   (a) has a significantly greater difficulty in learning than the majority of others of the same age, or
>   (b) has a disability which prevents or hinders him or her from making use of facilities of a kind generally provided for others of the same age in mainstream schools or mainstream post-16 institutions.
>
> (3) A child ... does not just have a learning difficulty or disability because the language (or form of language) in which he or she is (or will be) taught is different from a language (or form of language) which is (or is to be) spoken at home."

**The legal test for EHCNA (s.36(8)):** the LA must consider whether "the child or young person has or may have special educational needs" AND whether "it may be necessary for special educational provision to be made for the child or young person in accordance with an EHC plan".

**URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/20 ; https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6/section/36. **Status:** STATUTORY.

### A2.2 WALES — ALN definition (verbatim, ALN Act 2018 s.2(1)–(4))

> "(1) A person has additional learning needs if he or she has a learning difficulty or disability (whether or not he or she has any other learning difficulty or disability) which calls for additional learning provision to be made for him or her.
>
> (2) A person has a learning difficulty or disability if he or she —
>   (a) has a significantly greater difficulty in learning than the majority of others of the same age, or
>   (b) has a disability which prevents or hinders him or her from making use of facilities of a kind generally provided for others of the same age in mainstream schools or mainstream post-16 institutions."

**The legal test (s.2(3)–(4)):** additionally, the LA must decide that the person does **not** merely have a difficulty in learning which is solely attributable to a language other than Welsh or English (if Welsh-medium is the language of the school).

**URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2018/2/section/2. **Status:** STATUTORY.

### A2.3 SCOTLAND — ASN definition (verbatim, asp 4 s.1(1), as amended)

> "In this Act, 'additional support needs' means, in relation to a child or young person of school age, such needs (whether or not those needs arise out of any physical, mental, communication, behavioural, emotional, environmental or other cause) as call for the provision of additional support to enable the child or young person to benefit from school education."

**Key inclusions added by s.8 ASL Act 2009:**
- "for whatever reason" — broadens the categories
- "looked-after children" (looked-after under the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009)
- "looked-after" definitions under s.2(1A)–(1B)

**The legal test for a CSP (asp 4 s.2(2)):** the child/young person requires additional support from **education + one or more other agencies** to benefit from school education, the needs are **likely to continue for more than one year**, and they **significantly** affect the child's ability to benefit from school education.

**URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2004/4/section/1 ; https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/7/section/8. **Status:** STATUTORY.

### A2.4 NORTHERN IRELAND — SEN definition (Code 1.4)

> "A child has special educational needs if they have a learning difficulty or disability which calls for special educational provision to be made for him or her."

**The legal test (1996 Order art.5):** the Board (now EA) must identify a child as having SEN if they have a learning difficulty **greater than that of the majority of children of the same age** OR a disability which prevents use of mainstream facilities, AND which calls for special educational provision.

**URL:** https://www.education-ni.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2025-10/Code%20of%20Practice%20on%20the%20Identification%20and%20Assessment%20of%20Special%20Educational%20Needs.pdf (NI SEN CoP Oct 2025). **Status:** STATUTORY (Order) + STATUTORY GUIDANCE (Code).

## A5 — IMPLEMENTATION NOTES FOR ROUTINESTARS

1. **The 4-nation data model must include a `nation` enum** (ENGLAND / WALES / SCOTLAND / NORTHERN_IRELAND) and a `plan_type` enum (EHCP / IDP / CSP / STATEMENT) — these are not interchangeable.
2. **Wales IDP has a 35-school-day / 35-term-day / 7-week / 12-week timing family** (see Part B / Part E for the full detail) — implement as a configurable timer per IDP type.
3. **Scotland CSPs are rare** (only for the most complex cases) — most Scottish children will have ASN support without a CSP. Use a `support_type` enum to distinguish.
4. **NI is in transition** — support both legacy (Statement) and new (PLP) structures, with a `plan_status` enum indicating which framework the child is under.
5. **The England 4 areas map directly to canonical enums**; Wales, Scotland, NI have variations that need a small cross-walk table.
6. **The legal test for EHCNA / IDP / CSP / Statement differs subtly** — but all four are about (a) significantly greater difficulty than the majority AND/OR (b) a disability hindering access to facilities, AND (c) the need calls for **special/additional provision** beyond what is usually provided.
7. **The "looked-after children" category is cross-nation** — but the specifics differ (LAC = Children Act 1989 in England/Wales/NI; "looked-after" defined in the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009 in Scotland; "LAC" used in Wales under Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014).
8. **GIRFEC (Scotland) is a parallel but linked framework** — the "Named Person" and "Lead Professional" duties apply even where there is no CSP, and a CSP sits within the wider GIRFEC structure.
9. **NI's SEND Act 2016 framework is the future** — track commencement. The transition from Statement to PLP + LSCo will happen in waves; RoutineStars should support both.
10. **The Codes are statutory guidance, not statute** — but courts and tribunals are required to "have regard" to them; failure to do so without good reason is unlawful.

## A7 — CAVEATS AND GAPS

1. **Scotland 4th edition Code is in consultation** — re-check before production schema is locked.
2. **NI SEND Act 2016 commencement is staged** — re-check live status before production.
3. **The 4-nation area-of-need enums do not map one-to-one** — implement a canonical enum with a cross-walk table (A3.5).
4. **Wales and NI have specific LAC provisions** not fully expanded here — see Part F for the looked-after children variants.
5. **The "looked-after" definitions differ subtly** between nations — implement as a `lac_status` enum per nation.
6. **The "Tribunal" terms differ** — First-tier Tribunal (SEND) in England; Education Tribunal for Wales (ETW); Additional Support Needs Tribunal for Scotland (ASNTS); Special Educational Needs and Disability Tribunal (NI) (SENDIST).
7. **Some Codes have supplementary chapters** (e.g. NI 1998 Code Supplement 2005) — not separately itemised here; the primary Code of Practice is the entry point.
8. **Northern Ireland's Code is named "Code of Practice on the Identification and Assessment of Special Educational Needs"** — the **provision** code is a separate document (NI Code of Practice on the **Provision** of Special Educational Needs, also from DE NI).
