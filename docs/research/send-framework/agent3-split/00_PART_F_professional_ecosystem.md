# PART F -- Multi-Professional SEND / ALN / ASN Ecosystem across the 4 UK Nations

**Audience:** RoutineStars product, engineering, data, and clinical-safety leads.
**Purpose:** Define the externally-scopeable role set, the minimum data each role needs and produces, and the recognised multi-agency models that govern information sharing in SEND/ALN/ASN in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.
**Status convention used in this document:**
- **STATUTORY** -- duty created by primary or secondary legislation (an Act, Order, Regulations).
- **STATUTORY GUIDANCE** -- code of practice or guidance issued under statute that bodies named in the legislation *must* have regard to.
- **GOOD PRACTICE** -- guidance from a professional body, government, or recognised programme that is non-statutory but widely followed.

Where a section is national-only it is flagged; default is **UK-wide** unless a nation is named.
