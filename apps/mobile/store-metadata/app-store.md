### Full Description (4000 chars max)
RoutineStars helps children aged 4–14 — especially those with autism or special educational needs — build daily independence through fun, visual routines.

**For children:**
• Visual, step-by-step guides for morning, school, and bedtime routines
• Countdown timers that inform (never stress) your child
• Earn stars ⭐ and badges 🏆 for completing activities
• Build streaks 🔥 and celebrate every achievement
• Audio narration — no reading required
• Big, easy-to-tap buttons designed for little hands

**For parents:**
• Build a weekly schedule from a library of 15+ activity sets
• Approve activity completions with photo evidence
• Get instant push notifications when your child finishes a task
• Track progress with daily completion charts and weekly heatmaps
• Export PDF progress reports to share with schools or therapists
• Manage multiple children and invite care team members

**Privacy & Safety:**
• Children do not have their own accounts — all data is controlled by parents
• No advertising, no social features, no third-party data sharing
• COPPA and UK GDPR compliant
• All data encrypted in transit and at rest

**Plans:**
• Free: 1 child, 5 activity sets
• Starter (£7.99/mo): up to 3 children, all 15 sets, PDF reports
• Family (£14.99/mo): unlimited children, custom sets, care team sharing
• School (£49/mo): up to 30 children, teacher portal, bulk import
